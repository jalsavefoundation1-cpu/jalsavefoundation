WATER EDUCATION

Understanding Hard Water

Hard water generally contains higher concentrations of dissolved calcium and magnesium.

Hardness can create household and infrastructure challenges, especially when mineral deposits accumulate over time.

01

Scale deposits

Minerals may build up on taps, pipes and appliances.

02

Appliance maintenance

Mineral scale may affect water-heating equipment.

03

Soap performance

Hard water can reduce soap lathering efficiency.

WATER KNOWLEDGE

Mineral Water: Know the Facts

Natural mineral water may contain naturally occurring minerals such as calcium and magnesium.

Mineral content is only one part of water quality. Safe drinking water should be assessed using appropriate physical, chemical and microbiological parameters.

Important

Mineral content alone does not prove that water is safe to drink.
